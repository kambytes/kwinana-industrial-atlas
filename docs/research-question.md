Primary research question
How do reported industrial air-emission patterns, ambient-monitoring coverage, and population proximity vary across the Kwinana industrial area and surrounding communities?

Then record the four supporting questions:
1. Which facilities and pollutants account for the largest shares of reported annual air emissions in the Kwinana study area?
2. How have pollutant-specific reporting patterns changed over time?
3. Which population areas are closest to facilities reporting each selected pollutant?
4. Which selected pollutants are represented by nearby public ambient monitors, and how complete are those observations?
5. Where does the public evidence remain incomplete or non-comparable without project-specific dispersion modelling, emissions monitoring, or exposure assessment?

MVP scope:
- Kwinana industrial area + surrounding 5–10 km community context
- 2010 through the latest mutually usable period
- air emissions only
- NPI + DWER + ABS
- maximum three pollutants
- static Quarto report / GitHub Pages
