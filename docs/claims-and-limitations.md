Valid claims
For example, we can say:
- A named pollutant's reported annual emissions increased or decreased.
- A particular facility accounts for a specified share of reported annual emissions for that pollutant.
- A population area is within a specified distance of facilities reporting a pollutant.
- A monitor measures a particular pollutant.
- A monitor has a particular level of observation completeness.
- Public evidence is incomplete for a particular question.

Invalid claims
We must explicitly prohibit statements implying:
- NPI emissions = ambient concentration.
- Distance = pollutant transport.
- Distance = exposure.
- Facility = cause of a measured event.
- A facility is compliant/non-compliant.
- A community has elevated health risk.
- One pollutant is more harmful because its reported mass is larger.
- A site is suitable/unsuitable for development.

Our core implementation rule
This should be prominently recorded:
Every pollutant is analysed independently.

- We can compare patterns across pollutants, but we do not add kilograms of different pollutants together to create a "total pollution" score.
- That rule is particularly important because the NPI itself cautions against interpreting aggregated masses of different substances as a meaningful total-pollution measure.
