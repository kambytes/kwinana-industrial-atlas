# Kwinana Air Context

A reproducible public-data study of industrial air emissions, ambient monitoring, and community proximity in the Kwinana industrial area of Western Australia.

> **Status: Work in progress**

This project integrates public data from the National Pollutant Inventory (NPI), Western Australian Department of Water and Environmental Regulation (DWER), and the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) to investigate how reported industrial air-emission patterns, ambient-monitoring coverage, and population proximity vary across the Kwinana study area.

The project is currently in the setup and data-feasibility stage.

## Scope

The MVP focuses on:

- reported annual industrial air emissions;
- pollutant-specific analysis;
- ambient-air monitoring coverage;
- population proximity to reporting facilities; and
- transparent documentation of public-evidence limitations.
